# RAHUUL_RADAR Enterprise v2.0 — User Manual

Welcome to RAHUUL_RADAR Enterprise v2.0. This manual guides traders through using the Mobile Trading Dashboard, Swing Scanner, F&O Derivatives Scanner, Paper Trading Engine, Portfolio Manager, and Quant Reports.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview

The **Mobile Trading Dashboard** (`mobile/dashboard/`) provides institutional-grade visibility into your market operations:
- **Market Status Card:** Live NIFTY/BANKNIFTY trend, India VIX, and regime indicators.
- **Account Summary Card:** Total Equity, Cash Balance, Buying Power, Todays P&L, Total P&L, Max Drawdown.
- **Watchlists Tab:** Swing Momentum, F&O Options, and Intraday setups.
- **Risk Meter:** Real-time Portfolio Heat score (0-100), Value at Risk (VaR), Margin Utilization.
- **Analytics & Heatmap:** Monthly Return curve and strategy breakdown.
- **Notification Center:** Real-time AI alerts and system event logs.

---

## 2. Swing & F&O Scanners

- **Swing Scanner:** Automatically scans NSE stocks for high-probability swing trade setups. Signals include entry price, stop-loss, target 1, target 2, target 3, and AI confidence score (0-100).
- **F&O Derivatives Scanner:** Computes option chain greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho), IV Rank, PCR, Max Pain, and OI buildup to rank option buying and selling strategies across Weekly & Monthly expiries.

---

## 3. Paper Trading Platform

- **100% Virtual Trading:** Execute virtual orders (`BUY`, `SELL`, `MARKET`, `LIMIT`, `STOP`) without risking capital.
- **Virtual Capital:** Starts with ₹1,000,000 virtual balance.
- **Automated Trade Journal:** Every paper trade entry and exit is automatically saved with AI confidence, entry/exit reasons, risk/reward ratio, and screenshot references.
- **Signal Accuracy Validation:** Automatically compares AI signal predictions against actual price movement.

---

## 4. Reports & Quant Lab

- Access **Daily & Monthly Reports** directly from the dashboard to track Win Rate, Profit Factor, Sharpe Ratio, Expectancy, and Strategy Comparison.
